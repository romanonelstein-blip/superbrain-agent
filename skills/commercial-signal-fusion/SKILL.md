# Commercial Signal Fusion

## Workflow
1. Receive a user-authorized company or account scope and the approved ICP.
2. Retrieve only necessary facts from authorized sources; default to cached and free/read-only endpoints.
3. Normalize entity IDs, observed_at, event_at, source ID, event family, reliability and evidence hash.
4. Distinguish genuine recent change from a newly discovered old event. Mark missing baselines as UNKNOWN.
5. Deduplicate by workspace plus stable event fingerprint. Never match or expose cross-tenant private records by global contact key.
6. Score ICP fit, trigger relevance, freshness, provenance and independent corroboration as *review-priority estimates*, not proven purchase intent.
7. Run structured NEXUS evidence review where available; missing runtime or gates blocks approval status.
8. Recommend human account review, verification questions or a draft only. No unsolicited messaging or paid enrichment without separate budget, privacy, suppression and Master gates.
9. Report evidence-backed next action, counter-hypothesis, cost estimate and outcome measurement.

## Minimum standards
- No invented contacts, emails or date of role change.
- Never infer company growth without comparable snapshots.
- Never translate a positive score directly into permission to contact a person.
- Document API rate limits, credits and legal constraints before provider calls.

## Outputs
Typed observations, traceable score breakdown, confidence flags, human-review brief and clear BLOCKED/UNVERIFIED states.
